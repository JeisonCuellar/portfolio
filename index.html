Current Research

Master of Research

In Progress · Western Sydney University
The Hybrid Digital Agency Index (HDAI)

Australia became the first country to ban social media for children under 16 — but has no national measure of what digital capability actually looks like in its children, how it develops, or which groups are most at risk. This research builds that measure for the first time, combining longitudinal survey data, Latent Class Analysis, and cross-dataset validation to identify digital vulnerability profiles in Australian children aged 10–15.

Identifying Alcohol Vulnerability Profiles in Colombian Schoolchildren

Monthly alcohol prevalence among Colombian secondary school students jumped from 29% to 55% between 2008 and 2015 — but without knowing which students were most at risk and what drove their behaviour, prevention couldn't be targeted. This research built that picture for the first time across two national surveys (n=14,317).

Real · Carbon Positive Australia
Rebuilding Reporting Trust After a Data Integration Failure

The problem: Carbon Positive Australia's revenue and conversion reporting was unreliable — WooCommerce and Google Analytics were out of sync, meaning the team was making fundraising and marketing decisions based on data nobody could fully trust. The root cause had never been investigated.

What I did: Conducted a full cross-system audit reconciling transaction counts, revenue totals, and event tracking between GA4 and WooCommerce. Identified the integration sync issues causing the discrepancies, validated the corrected data, and built reusable reporting templates the team could use independently — so the fix lasted beyond the engagement.

The outcome: The organisation gained a single trusted data source for the first time, reducing manual reporting effort by ~40% and giving leadership the confidence to make decisions based on accurate numbers.

Real · Nielsen Colombia
Protecting Stakeholder Trust During a Methodology Migration

The problem: When Nielsen migrated its data collection methodology, historical comparisons risked breaking — meaning major FMCG clients could make commercial decisions based on unreliable trend data. The challenge was to validate the new model against the old, identify where discrepancies existed, and keep reporting trustworthy throughout the transition.

What I did: Built automated ANOVA and regression workflows in R and SQL that ran parallel validation between both methodologies — scaling model output from 30 to 80/day. Used VBA macros to clean and prepare data, expanding coverage across food product categories and enabling the team to deliver consistent forecasts to the data science team without interruption.

The outcome: Stakeholders received continuous, trustworthy reporting throughout the transition. No decisions were made on unreliable data.

Real · Carbon Positive Australia
Donor Segmentation: Building a Communication Framework for CPOA

The problem: Carbon Positive Australia was struggling to maintain and grow donor engagement — not because donors weren't interested, but because CPOA had no way to understand who they were communicating with. Without a clear picture of their donor base, every message went to everyone the same way, making it impossible to communicate accurately or meaningfully.

What I did: Analysed the donor CRM database using Python and R to build structured profiles based on donation behaviour, frequency, recency, and motivation. Classified donors into distinct segments — each with a clear identity, engagement pattern, and communication need — giving CPOA the tool they were missing to understand exactly who they were talking to.

The outcome: CPOA gained a clear segmentation framework that allowed them to tailor their messaging to each donor type — moving from one-size-fits-all communication to targeted, accurate outreach that matched each donor's motivations and relationship with the organisation.
